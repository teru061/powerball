      Pricing

      Each buy pushes that team's price up and the others down, so backing the favourite costs more. Prices across live teams always add to 100.

      Payout

      When the champion is crowned, each of their shares pays 100 credits. Shares in other teams pay nothing. Highest net worth wins bragging rights.
